About this tool

An open, frontend-only dashboard to explore and download key indicators from India's National Family Health Survey (NFHS) across four rounds — NFHS-3 (2005–06), NFHS-4 (2015–16), NFHS-5 (2019–21) and the newly released NFHS-6 (2023–24) — for India and all states/UTs, at Urban / Rural / Total levels. Everything runs in your browser; no data leaves your device and no server is required.

Disclaimer

This tool presents official NFHS fact-sheet estimates compiled for convenience; it is not produced or endorsed by IIPS or the Government of India. Values are survey estimates subject to sampling and non-sampling error and are presented without significance testing. Indicator definitions changed across rounds, so cross-round comparisons may not be like-for-like — check the “Comparable / Definition changed” flag and the definitions shown with each indicator before drawing conclusions. Figures here may differ from the source fact sheets due to extraction or rounding; the original IIPS fact sheets are authoritative. This tool deliberately presents data without interpretation. It is not intended for clinical, legal, financial, or commercial decision-making. Map boundaries are for visual reference only and imply no position on the status of any boundary. Provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind.
Important data notes — read before publishing.
  • Manipur is absent from NFHS-6 (fieldwork not completed); it appears in earlier rounds.
  • Boundary changes: Ladakh appears only from NFHS-5; Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu are separate UTs in NFHS-3/4 but a single merged UT in NFHS-5/6, so those do not align across rounds.
  • Definitions shifted across rounds (age groups, denominators, vaccine schedules). Harmonised series are indicative; verify definitions in the source fact-sheet footnotes before citing exact cross-round changes.
  • ▲ flags estimates shown in parentheses in NFHS-5 (based on small/unweighted samples).
  • Map boundaries are derived from an open community GeoJSON for visual reference only and are not an authoritative depiction of international or state borders.

Data sources

Compiled from the official IIPS fact sheets: NFHS-6 (2023–24) extracted from the IIPS Fact Sheets PDF (released May 2026); NFHS-5, NFHS-4 and NFHS-3 from the published fact-sheet data tables. Underlying surveys conducted by the International Institute for Population Sciences (IIPS), Mumbai, for the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India.
