Explore India's National Family Health Survey,
NFHS-3 to NFHS-6 — in one place.

An open, free tool to explore and download key health, nutrition and demographic indicators across four survey rounds (2005–06 to 2023–24) for India and every state & UT — on an interactive map, as trends over time, and as downloadable data. Everything runs in your browser.

Open the Dashboard → How to use

What you can do

🗺️

Map any indicator

Colour every state/UT by a chosen indicator and round. States without data stay visible in grey; values can be printed on the downloaded image.

📈

See trends & compare

Track an indicator across all four rounds for any set of states, or rank every state for one round.

⬇️

Download & share

Export any view as CSV, PNG or the full Excel/CSV/Parquet dataset — or copy the URL to share an exact view.

How to use it — in 4 steps

  1. Pick an indicator group, then the indicator (the list shows only what that round measured).
  2. Choose the round (NFHS-3 to 6) and the area — Total, Urban or Rural.
  3. Read the ✓ comparable / ⚠ definition-changed flag and the per-round definitions before comparing across rounds.
  4. Download the data or image, or copy the URL to share that exact view with anyone.

Good to know

This is a derived compilation of official NFHS fact-sheet estimates — not produced or endorsed by IIPS or the Government of India. Estimates carry survey error and are shown without significance testing.
  • Manipur is absent from NFHS-6 (field-work not completed); it appears in earlier rounds.
  • Indicator definitions changed across some rounds — cross-round comparisons may not be like-for-like (see the comparability flags).
  • The original IIPS fact sheets are authoritative. Not for clinical, legal, financial or commercial decisions. Map boundaries are illustrative only.
